## Launch decision

No broad local supplements are fabricated without actual applicability facts. The cookie disclosures remain in Privacy section /privacy#cookies rather than a duplicate policy. Confirm launch countries and relevant language/notice requirements, then prepare targeted supplements/representatives/contracts where required. If operations cannot satisfy a market's obligations, assess an explicit restriction or geographic control before accepting those users; no control was added or claimed in this patch. England-and-Wales governing law cannot waive mandatory foreign protections.
